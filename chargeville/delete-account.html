Chargeville — Delete your account

Chargeville is published by EV Station Games (Muhammad Azizullo Gulomov).

What the account is

Chargeville creates a game account only when you sign in with Google Play Games (Android) or Game Center (iOS). The account stores your progress in the cloud so it comes back when you reinstall the game or play on another device. If you never sign in, no account exists and nothing is stored outside your device.

Delete it inside the game (fastest)

  1. Open Chargeville.
  2. Tap the Menu button, then Settings.
  3. Scroll to the bottom of the settings list.
  4. Tap Delete account.
  5. Confirm. The account and the cloud save are deleted straight away, and the game starts a new save on the device.

The Delete account row only appears when you are signed in with Play Games or Game Center, because there is nothing to delete otherwise.

Ask us to delete it by email

If you no longer have the game installed, email us and we will delete the account for you. Send the message from the address linked to your Play Games or Game Center account, write Delete my Chargeville account in the subject, and include the player name shown in Play Games or Game Center.

gulomovmazizullo@gmail.com

We reply within 2 business days and complete the deletion within 30 days.

What is deleted, what is kept

DataWhat happens
Account identifier (the game account linked to Play Games or Game Center) Deleted immediately
Cloud save: station level, money, buildings, upgrades and other game progress Deleted immediately
The save stored on your device Replaced by a new, empty save when you confirm in the game. Deleting the app removes it as well.
Analytics and crash reports (device model, app version, in-game events) Not linked to your account and not used to identify you. Google deletes crash reports after 90 days and analytics records after 14 months at the latest.
Purchases Held by Google Play or Apple, not by us. They stay with your store account so you can restore them, and we cannot delete them.
Advertising identifier used for ads Belongs to your device. You can reset or delete it in your device settings at any time.

Deletion cannot be undone. Once the cloud save is gone, the progress cannot be recovered, so claim anything you want to keep before you delete.
